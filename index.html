2023–24 CRDC public-use file is still awaiting release
Open for the full alert, why it matters, next reporting move and official sources.
DATAFOLLOW-UP

Full update

The U.S. Department of Education has posted the 2023–24 Civil Rights Data Collection cycle on its website, but the full downloadable public-use file is not yet available. The federal download table still shows the 2023–24 bulk file as unavailable, while OCR says it expects to release the public-use file in September 2026.

Once the file drops, the strongest Massachusetts use is not merely a statewide summary. It is a district- and school-level anomaly scan: suspension disparities by race and disability; restraint and seclusion; access to advanced coursework; school policing; counselors and support staff; and changes from 2021–22.

Why it matters

CRDC is one of the few national datasets that lets you compare civil-rights indicators across virtually every public school and district using consistent measures. It is especially useful for identifying places where the disparity itself is the reporting lead.

Next reporting move

Keep the release watch active. When the file appears, download Massachusetts records, clean school and district identifiers, calculate disparity ratios and flag extreme values for reporting rather than treating the rankings as conclusions on their own.

FOLLOW-UP

Full update

The Alpha Massachusetts watch is focused on concrete new developments rather than repeated background: local approvals and permitting; public-school pilots and Springfield Empowerment Zone actions; contracts and procurement; parent communications and opt-in or opt-out practices; student privacy and surveillance; lawsuits; corporate registrations and ownership; lobbying; and ties among Alpha-related entities and programs.

Why it matters

The most useful alerts are likely to come from local records that show what Alpha or related organizations are actually doing in Massachusetts, especially where public students, public dollars, student data or local governance are involved.

Next reporting move

Prioritize primary documents. Each substantive future alert will include direct links to the agenda packet, contract, court filing, corporate record or other source that generated the alert.
